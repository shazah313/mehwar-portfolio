Our Journey Through Time

From four founders in Ernakulam to a product trusted by hundreds of thousands.

2025 Four founders united in Ernakulam, Kerala
In March 2025, Mohammed Shafeeque, Mohammed Raahil, Muhsin Shareef P.L, and Muhammad Anwar A.K founded Mehwar Technology — united by a passion to build technology that serves communities and businesses at scale.
2025 AlBishara — our first product launches
AlBishara Masjid Management App became our flagship — a complete digital platform for prayer times, announcements, member management, and donations on Android, iOS, Web, and Windows.
2025 600,000+ active users on AlBishara
AlBishara surpassed 600,000 active users, proving that Mehwar Technology can build products that scale and genuinely transform how communities connect and manage their masjid operations.
2025 Partnerships with leading organizations
Alongside AlBishara's growth, we delivered custom solutions for Mr. SI Consultancy, P. A. Polytechnic College Mangalore, and more — expanding across mobile, web, robotics, and AI services.
Today 12 services · growing every day
Today Mehwar Technology offers twelve technology domains — from Flutter and MERN to robotics, AI, and cloud. Follow our journey on YouTube as we continue shaping tomorrow's technology, today.
