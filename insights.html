Our First Product · 600K+ Users

AlBishara Masjid Management App

AlBishara is Mehwar Technology's first and flagship product — a complete masjid management system launched in March 2025. Designed to help mosques manage prayer times, announcements, member records, donations, and community communication, AlBishara brings essential mosque operations into one easy-to-use digital platform.

Built with Flutter and Firebase, the app runs seamlessly on Android, iOS, Web, and Windows — ensuring every community member can stay connected regardless of their device. The team's focus on reliability, simplicity, and cultural sensitivity made AlBishara the go-to choice for masjid committees across the region.

Today, AlBishara proudly serves 600,000+ active users, standing as proof of Mehwar Technology's ability to build products that scale and make a real difference in people's daily lives.

Vehicles & Properties

Mr. SI Consultancy

Mr. SI Consultancy — specialists in vehicles and properties — approached Mehwar Technology to digitize their client management and advisory workflows. They needed a professional platform to track consultations, manage documents, and present insights to their business clients — all from a single dashboard.

We built a custom web application using React.js and Laravel, featuring secure client portals, appointment scheduling, document vaults, and automated reporting. The MERN-inspired frontend paired with a robust Laravel API delivered the performance and security their consultancy demanded.

Since launch, Mr. SI Consultancy has reduced manual paperwork by 70% and improved client response times significantly — allowing their team to focus on what they do best: delivering expert business guidance.

Import & Export · Kasargod

Al Anfal Global LLP

Al Anfal Global LLP is an independent import and export company based at Smart Tower, Uppala, Mangalpady, Kasargod, Kerala. Trading as ANFAL GLOBAL, the firm connects quality products with international markets through sourcing, inspection, and dependable trade relationships — and is a separate business from other similarly named companies worldwide.

The company moves imported drinks and beverages, imported fruits, coconut oil, virgin coconut oil, and spices from growers and suppliers to buyers. Kenya is an important part of that network. Every shipment follows the same path: source, select, trade, and deliver — from origin, through port and freight, to the market. Founder Mohammad Fahad Kotta leads the business, with Mufazi Rahman Kotta as director. The firm is registered as ALAnfal GLOBAL LLP (LLPIN ADA-6616, GSTIN 32ACPFA4276PIZT).

Mehwar Technology partnered with Al Anfal Global to present this trade business online, so buyers and suppliers can see what they trade, how the supply chain runs, and how to start a conversation — from Kasargod to global ports.

Education

P. A. Polytechnic, Mangalore (PAPT)

P. A. Polytechnic (PAPT), Nadupadav, Mangalore — an AICTE-approved institution affiliated to BTE Bangalore — partnered with Mehwar Technology to modernize their digital presence and streamline academic operations for students and faculty.

We developed a Flutter-based mobile application and responsive web portal enabling students to access timetables, attendance records, announcements, and course materials from any device. The admin dashboard gives faculty real-time control over content, notifications, and student data.

The solution has strengthened PAPT Mangalore's commitment to technical education excellence, giving their students a seamless digital campus experience aligned with modern polytechnic standards.
